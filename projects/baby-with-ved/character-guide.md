# Baby With Ved — Character Bible

Use this guide as the visual source of truth for every episode and scene.

## Ved — main character
- Age: 3-year-old toddler.
- Hair: soft, fluffy, curly black/dark-brown hair.
- Eyes: large, expressive brown eyes.
- Personality: cheerful, curious, friendly, energetic.
- Fixed outfit: bright yellow short-sleeve T-shirt, blue denim dungarees, red sneakers.
- Proportions: toddler body, rounded cheeks, soft rounded 3D animation shapes.

## Pip — companion character
- Species: tiny bird (NOT a dog or puppy).
- Feathers: turquoise / sky-blue.
- Belly: bright yellow.
- Beak and feet: orange.
- Eyes: large and expressive.
- Personality: friendly, funny, playful, loves dancing.

## Visual continuity rules
- Preserve the same face, age, body proportions, hair, clothing, colors, and accessories in every scene.
- Preserve Pip as the same small turquoise-blue bird with a yellow belly and orange beak/feet.
- Do not turn Pip into a dog, puppy, or any other animal.
- Do not change Ved's clothes, hairstyle, eye color, or age between shots.
- Original 3D family-animation look, soft rounded shapes, vibrant pastel colors, warm cinematic lighting.
- Use front-facing character references and the same reference image in every generation workflow that supports image references.
- Avoid copyrighted characters, brand logos, watermarks, distorted faces, extra limbs, inconsistent character designs, and flickering.
