ORIGINAL SONG SCRIPT

Lyrics and actions

Opening

Good morning, good morning,
Hello sunshine, hello day!
उठ जाओ वेद प्यारे,
Come on out and smile and play!

Verse 1 — Wake up

Stretch your arms up to the sky,
Wiggle fingers, blink your eyes.
सूरज आया, रोशनी लाई,
नई सुबह खुशियाँ लाई!

Chorus — Clap and smile

Clap, clap, clap your hands,
Turn around and dance, dance, dance!
हँसो ज़रा और ताली बजाओ,
वेद के संग सब मुस्कुराओ!

Verse 2 — Get ready

Wash your face and brush, brush, brush,
Take your time, there's no need to rush.
मुँह धो लो और दाँत चमकाओ,
साफ़-सुथरे बनकर आओ!

Final chorus

Good morning, good morning, what a lovely day,
Learn and laugh and sing and play!
वेद बोले, आओ भाई,
हर सुबह है नई मिठाई!

Production note: Keep the tune, backing track, sound effects, and character voices original. Lyrics are a draft and can be refined during music production.
