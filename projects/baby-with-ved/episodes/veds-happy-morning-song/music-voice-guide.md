# Episode 001 — Music, Voice and Audio Production Guide

## Creative direction
- Mood: joyful, gentle, sunny, playful.
- Audience: preschool children and families.
- Language: simple English + Hindi.
- Tempo target: upbeat but easy for children to clap along, approximately 100–112 BPM.
- Arrangement: original melody, light ukulele or acoustic guitar, marimba/bell accents, hand claps, soft bass, gentle percussion.
- Keep vocals clear and comfortably above the backing track.
- Create an entirely original melody and instrumental; do not imitate or sample an existing nursery rhyme or recording.

## Vocal direction
- Lead vocal: warm, friendly, smiling delivery, clear pronunciation, moderate pace.
- English and Hindi lines should have similar energy and easy sing-along phrasing.
- Add a small group of soft childlike backing responses only if the voice provider has licensed synthetic voices suitable for this use.
- Do not clone a real child's voice or any real person's voice without explicit consent.

## Suggested recording sections
1. Opening: 0:00–0:25
2. Wake-up verse: 0:25–0:55
3. Clap-and-dance chorus: 0:55–1:25
4. Wash-and-brush verse: 1:25–1:55
5. Instrumental / get-ready transition: 1:55–2:25
6. Final sing-along chorus: 2:25–3:05
7. Goodbye tag: 3:05–3:25

## Sound effects
- Soft morning birds at the opening.
- Gentle curtain / window ambience in the sunshine scene.
- Light hand-clap accents aligned to the chorus.
- Quiet water sound in the wash scene.
- Tiny playful bird chirps for Pip, kept low under the vocals.
- Soft sparkle chime on the end title.

## Mix and export
- Avoid sudden loud sounds and sharp peaks.
- Keep dialogue and singing intelligible on phone speakers.
- Export a clean WAV master for editing, then AAC audio inside the final MP4.
- Check the mix on phone speakers and headphones before publishing.
