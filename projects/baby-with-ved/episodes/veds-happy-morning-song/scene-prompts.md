## Scene 1 — Wake up (00:00–00:25)
Inside a cozy colorful child's bedroom at sunrise. Ved wakes, stretches both arms, smiles, and waves. Pip the tiny turquoise-blue bird flutters onto the bedpost and chirps happily. Start with a wide establishing shot, then a gentle camera push-in to Ved's happy face. Calm, warm morning light.

## Scene 2 — Hello sunshine (00:25–00:55)
Ved walks to the window and greets the rising sun. Pip flies in a small cheerful circle near the window, then lands safely on a nearby perch. Golden sunbeams, soft curtains moving gently, medium shot followed by a wide view of the bright morning outside.

## Scene 3 — Clap and dance (00:55–01:25)
In the bedroom, Ved claps a simple rhythm, takes two small side steps, and turns around. Pip dances with tiny hops and wing flutters beside him. Full-body framing so young viewers can copy the actions. Smooth rhythmic camera movement.

## Scene 4 — Wash and brush (01:25–01:55)
A clean child-friendly bathroom. Ved washes his face at a safe child-height sink, then brushes his teeth. Pip watches from a nearby shelf and cheers with a happy wing flap. Bright clean pastel setting, gentle medium shots, no unsafe actions.

## Scene 5 — Get ready (01:55–02:25)
Ved tidies his bed and gets ready for the day while keeping his signature yellow T-shirt, blue dungarees, and red sneakers unchanged. Pip helps by playfully nudging a small toy into its basket. Show teamwork and simple positive habits.

## Scene 6 — Sing and play (02:25–03:05)
A sunny garden with flowers, trees, and a safe open lawn. Ved and Pip perform the final sing-along dance: clap, wave, two steps, and a happy turn. Use a wide shot, then a medium shot with both characters clearly visible. Keep Pip's bird anatomy and turquoise/yellow/orange colors exact.

## Scene 7 — Goodbye wave (03:05–03:25)
Ved and Pip stand together in the sunny garden and wave to the camera. Pip flutters briefly beside Ved's shoulder. End with a warm happy smile and a clean open area in frame for the Baby With Ved title to be added during editing. Slow gentle pull-back.
