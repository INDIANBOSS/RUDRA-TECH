SAMPLE WEEKLY CONTENT CALENDAR

Starter publishing plan

MONDAY · EDUCATE

Quick tip

One practical IT or productivity tip in a 20–30 second short video.

CTA: Save this tip.

WEDNESDAY · ENGAGE

Question or poll

Ask the audience about a common tech problem or favourite nursery rhyme topic.

CTA: Comment your answer.

FRIDAY · SHOWCASE

Behind the scenes

Show a safe work process, a creative production step, or a before/after workflow.

CTA: Follow for the next update.

SUNDAY · REVIEW

Weekly recap

Review the best-performing post and write down one improvement for next week.

CTA: Share with someone who needs it.

PUBLISHING CHECKLIST

Before every post

Confirm platform format, audience, hook, spelling, captions, thumbnail, rights to music and visuals, links, and the final approval. After publishing, record the URL and review performance after a consistent period.
